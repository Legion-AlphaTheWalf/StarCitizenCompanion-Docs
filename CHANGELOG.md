## 3.4.1 — October 8, 2026

- Start trading, search for an item, or browse ships directly from Home. Open your personal settings from the activity menu.
- Follow community note search results into the full note, then go back to the list. Empty searches keep the activity menu available.
- Browse all pages of crew activities and return from jobs and earnings. Closed activities keep their navigation with participation controls disabled.
- Read long ship details across multiple pages and return to your filtered ship list.
- Crafting material estimates now account for recipes that produce several items at once. Mining trip estimates correctly round up fractional cargo amounts.
- Fixed menu updates and cleanup so an older timer cannot remove your current result. Use **Close** to remove a menu or **Keep result** to leave its card and source links. Shared hubs and crew boards stay in place.
- Find UEX player sellers from weapons, materials, and recipe ingredients. Filter listings and open UEX to contact the trader. Player listings and NPC shop prices remain separate.
- Read these patch notes from **Home → What’s new**.
