## 3.4.0 — October 8, 2026

- Find UEX player sellers when browsing weapons, materials, and recipe ingredients. Player listings and NPC shop prices are shown separately.
- Browse sellers or buyers and filter by price, quality, location, currency, and unit. Open a listing on UEX to contact the trader.
- Searches and topic changes now reuse your menu instead of filling the channel with new replies.
- Use **Close** to remove a menu or **Keep result** to save the card without buttons. Unused menus clear automatically; shared hubs and crew boards stay in place.
- More consistent cards across searches, settings, and crew jobs. Fixed Back navigation in ship and marketplace results.
- Read patch notes from **Home → What’s new**.
